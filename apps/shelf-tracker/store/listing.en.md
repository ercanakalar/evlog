# Google Play: English

## App name (30 at most)

Raf: Grocery Receipts & Expiry

## Short description (80 at most)

Scan grocery receipts, see your spending, use food before it goes off.

## Full description (4000 at most)

Raf reads your grocery receipts and keeps your kitchen in order: see what you spend, and use what you buy before it goes off.

SCAN YOUR RECEIPTS
• Take a photo or pick one from your gallery; the market, date, products and prices are read for you.
• Photograph long receipts in parts and Raf joins them.
• Check, fix or remove any line before saving.

USE IT IN TIME
• A countdown for every product shows what to use first.
• Read the date on the pack with the camera to replace the estimate.
• Get a reminder 1, 2 or 3 days before something runs out.

KNOW WHAT IS AT HOME
• Products on your receipts are added to your counts at home, by brand and pack size.
• What runs out goes on your market list, with the last price you paid.

SEE YOUR SPENDING
• Month by month, by category and by market.
• Compared with the same day last month.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Turkish and English, light and dark, eight colours and four font styles.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No ads, no tracking, and your data is not sold.
• Receipts are read by our own software, not by an outside recognition service.
• Delete your account and everything in it from the app.

Made for Turkish receipts. Countdowns are estimates: always go by the date on the pack and by how the food looks and smells.
